Exercise

Progressive hints

Key questions from the syllabus to answer on your own, with hints that are revealed one at a time: from the vaguest to the one that almost gives you the answer. If they run out, a text and an explanation help you get there.

Exercise

Logic Corner

Build truth tables, check whether an argument is valid, analyse syllogisms with Venn diagrams, discover how logic works inside a circuit and test yourself with paradoxes, exercises and classic arguments.

Exercise

Rewriting: saying it in your own words

Explaining a text in your own words is not copying it, nor swapping every word for a synonym, nor saying something else. Learn to tell real rewriting apart from those three traps and test yourself with passages from the great philosophers.

Exercise

Knots: do your reasons fit together?

Answer some statements and discover where your own answers clash. Each knot is untied by writing a reason: changing your mind, distinguishing the cases or accepting the consequence.
